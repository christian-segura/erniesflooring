Flooring FAQ

What is self-cove sheet vinyl?

Self-cove means the sheet vinyl runs a few inches up the wall in one continuous piece instead of using a separate rubber base. With no seam where the floor meets the wall, water and dirt can't get underneath. It's the standard for medical offices, restrooms, kitchens and labs.

Should I choose glue-down or click vinyl plank?

Glue-down plank is bonded to the subfloor, so it holds up best under heavy rolling loads, high traffic and large open areas. That's why it's preferred for commercial spaces. Click (floating) plank installs faster and works well in most homes. Ernie will recommend the right one after seeing the space.

Do I need a moisture barrier over concrete?

Concrete slabs release moisture that can cause adhesive failure, cupping, mold and odors. A moisture barrier protects the new floor, and manufacturers often require one for the warranty. Ernie checks the slab during the estimate.
